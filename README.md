# Supplementary Materials for "The History of Trauma and Resilience of the Mexican People in the United States"
This repository contains supplementary notes, drafts, and extended citations related to the published paper: "The History of Trauma and Resilience of the Mexican People in the United States". These materials offer additional context, nuances, and a more comprehensive list of references that may be useful for researchers and educators.

## Published Paper

**[[https://www.mdpi.com/2313-5778/5/2/32]](https://doi.org/10.3390/genealogy5020032)**

Please cite the published paper if you use the main work.

Note: UPDATED CITATION 

El Semillero de la Raza. (2021). The historical trauma and resilience of individuals of Mexican Ancestry in the United States: A scoping literature review and emerging conceptual framework. Genealogy, 5(2), 32.

## Purpose and Public Education

This repository is intended for public education and to provide a more in-depth look into the research process behind "The historical trauma and resilience of individuals of Mexican Ancestry in the United States: A scoping literature review and emerging conceptual framework." While the published paper provides the definitive account of our findings, these notes and drafts may offer additional insights and references that could be valuable to a broader audience.

**Important Note for Readers:** If you utilize any specific insights or citations found within these supplementary materials, **please cite the original cited work directly**. The purpose of this repository is to make these resources more accessible, not to be a primary citation for the referenced material.

  ## Citing the Cited Work

This repository includes numerous citations from the underlying research. For any specific information or references you find useful within these notes and drafts, **it is imperative that you cite the original authors and their respective publications directly.**

For example, if you use information from a note that references "Smith et al. (2020)", please look up the full citation for Smith et al. (2020) in the `references/citations.bib` file (or within the notes themselves if full citations are provided inline) and cite that work directly in your own research.

We have provided a comprehensive bibliography in `references/citations.bib` to facilitate this.
